# Code of Conduct - Videographr

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Videographr project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Examples of positive behaviour**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and research traditions
- Giving and accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for responsible video-based education research

**Unacceptable behaviour**

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sharing classroom video or identifiable research media without proper rights
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional research and education setting

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is representing
the project in public spaces (e.g. using an official project account).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour should be
reported through a monitored private conduct route named on the GitHub repository.
No separate conduct route was verified during the 2026-08-09 release review. Do
not publish sensitive details while that operational route is unavailable.
Maintainers must provision and test it before representing private conduct
reporting as available. Submitted complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.
